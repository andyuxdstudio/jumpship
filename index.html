        How it works

        Four steps take you from what you know to a career you have actually tried.

        1. Set your limits

          Hours and pay

          Tell us the lowest income you can accept and the most in-person hours you can work. Careers that break either limit never appear.

        2. Pick your skills

          What you do in UX

          Tap the work you really do: research, wireframes, testing, stakeholder management. Add your own if we missed it.

        3. See your ports

          Your top five matches

          Each match explains why it fits your skills and gives an honest reality check on pay, ways in and exposure to AI.

        4. Go ashore

          A taster of about two hours

          Try the career with a guided task. Finish by saying how it felt, and leave with one next step.

        Not sure where to start? Spin the wheel for a surprise. It only picks from careers that fit your limits.
